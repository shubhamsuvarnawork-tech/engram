The problem

Companies don't run on documents. They run on decisions.

Most of those decisions are undocumented — living in senior people's heads and buried Slack threads, and walking out the door the day they leave. Your AI agents inherit the model's intelligence, but none of your company's.

The system

A living model of how you operate.

Engram ingests your policies, tickets, and conversations and structures them into a knowledge-and-decision graph — every fact and rule scored for confidence and freshness, every edge traceable to its source.

Execution

Autonomous where it's safe. Human where it counts.

Every action is gated by confidence and guardrails. The agent resolves the obvious cases instantly and routes the rest to a human with the full context to decide in seconds — never a black box.

The loop

Every correction makes it sharper.

When a human overrides the agent, Engram captures it — re-weighting the decision and proposing an update to the brain for review. The system doesn't just store your knowledge; it compounds it. This is the moat that widens with every use.
